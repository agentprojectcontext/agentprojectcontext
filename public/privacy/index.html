Privacy policy

APX and Agent Project Context (APC) · Last updated October 22, 2026

APX is an open-source desktop app that runs AI agents on your own computer. APC is the open convention (AGENTS.md and .apc/) it implements. This policy explains what data APX handles and where it goes. The short version: APX runs on your machine, keeps your data there, and has no servers that receive your content.

Where your data lives

Your agents, chats, memory, files and settings are stored on the computer where you run APX. Passwords, keys and access tokens go to that computer's secret store (the system keychain where there is one). APX's authors do not run a service that receives them.

Google user data

You can connect your Google account to APX so your agents can work with Google Calendar, Gmail and Google Drive. Each part is connected on its own, and only after you approve it on Google's own consent page. With APX's sign-in, APX asks for:

  • Google Calendar (calendar.events, calendar.readonly): to read your events and free time, and to create, change or cancel the events you ask for, with invitations and Google Meet links.
  • Gmail (gmail.send): to send an email you asked for. Every message is shown to you and sent only after you approve it. APX does not read your mail with this access.
  • Google Drive (drive.file): to create Google Docs and share them with the people you name. APX reaches only the files it created.
  • Your email address (openid, email): to show you which account is connected.

How this data is used:

  • Google data is requested and processed on your computer, only to do what you asked your agent to do. The access token Google issues stays in your computer's secret store and is never sent to APX's authors.
  • When an agent reads an event or a file to answer you, that content goes to the AI model provider you configured in APX (for example Google Gemini, OpenAI or a local model), as part of your request, under that provider's terms. APX sends it nowhere else.
  • Google data is not sold, not used for advertising, not used to build user profiles, and not used to train or improve AI or machine learning models, by APX or by its authors.
  • APX's authors never see your Google data. No human reads it, except you.

APX's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

Disconnect a Google part in APX (Resources › Integrations) to delete its stored token from your computer. You can also revoke APX's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Everything else APX stores is in its folder on your computer; uninstalling APX and deleting that folder removes it.

Usage numbers

Only if you say yes, APX sends anonymous usage numbers (for example the app version and how many agents you run) so its authors can see how it is used. They never include your content, your messages, your files or any Google data. You can turn them off in APX at any time.

This website

agentprojectcontext.com is a static site. It sets no cookies of its own and runs no analytics.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. Questions: open an issue at github.com/agentprojectcontext/apx, or write to the support address shown on Google's consent screen for APX.
